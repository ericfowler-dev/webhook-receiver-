How the math works

  • Costs rise each year by the inflation rate you set, compounding from year 1.
  • Gift aid (grants and scholarships) is subtracted first. Untick "renews" for one-time awards.
  • Order of payment: gift aid → family contribution + student job income → savings/529 → loans cover the rest.
  • Every amount carries its own period. A monthly figure is converted to a year before anything is added to it, so a $500/month contribution and a $12,000/semester tuition bill never get mixed up.
  • In-school interest accrues on each year's borrowing from mid-year until graduation (unsubsidized-loan behavior).
  • Monthly payment is a standard amortized payment over the repayment term you set.
  • Salary strain is the monthly payment as a share of expected starting gross pay. Under 8% is comfortable, 8–12% is manageable, over 12% is tight.
  • Affordability score compares total lifetime cost to family; the cheapest school scores 10 and the rest scale by ratio.
